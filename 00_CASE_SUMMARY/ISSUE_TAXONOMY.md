## LEGAL ISSUES (claims and their elements)

### L1 — ADEA Disparate Treatment (age discrimination)
**Elements:** (1) member of protected class (40+); (2) qualified / meeting legitimate expectations; (3) suffered an adverse employment action; (4) circumstances raising an inference of age discrimination (e.g., replacement by or transfer of duties to substantially younger workers); **(5) age was the "but-for" cause.**
**Our position:** 74–75-year-old, 25+ yrs experience, "excellent" review; core role/duties stripped and transferred to Cape (28) and Wrenn; direct age remark by CFO; ultimately terminated with duties absorbed by Cape.
**Their position:** No adverse action (transfer = lateral, same pay/duties); role not assumed by a younger employee (Blackstream eliminated the role); cannot meet but-for burden.
**Related factual issues:** F1, F3, F4, F7, F8, F9, F21.

### L2 — ADEA Hostile Work Environment (age-based harassment)
**Elements:** (1) unwelcome conduct; (2) based on age; (3) sufficiently **severe or pervasive** to alter conditions; (4) basis to impute liability to employer.
**Our position:** Age disclosure by Cape; ageist remarks by Hall & James; overheard "too old…should just quit"; Aldridge's Super Bowl "over 40" remark; systematic marginalization over a sustained period.
**Their position:** Isolated/denied; Cape didn't know/disclose age; overheard remark misattributed and benign.
**Related factual issues:** F10, F11, F12, F19.

### L3 — ADEA Retaliation
**Elements:** (1) protected activity (opposition to discrimination / complaint); (2) materially adverse action; (3) causal link (often shown by temporal proximity / pretext).
**Our position:** Nov 14 complaint → verbal warning ~3 weeks later → termination during pendency of charges; shifting/pretextual explanations; selective enforcement.
**Their position:** Warning was for a legitimate reason (false payroll statements), learned ~3 weeks after complaint; same manager gave excellent review; warning not materially adverse.
**Related factual issues:** F13, F14, F15, F16, F17, F24.

### L4 — Constructive Discharge → Actual Discharge
**Elements (constructive):** working conditions so intolerable a reasonable person would feel compelled to resign. **Now superseded by actual termination (5/15/2026)** — the most serious adverse action.
**Our position:** Authority/budget/staff stripped; 90.3% non-response; office/equipment/meeting denials; then actually terminated, mooting Respondent's "still employed / could not be constructively discharged" defense.
**Their position:** Still employed in same position (as of 6/24/2026 statement) → no constructive discharge. **(This representation is now false; see L8.)**
**Related factual issues:** F4, F17, F19, F26.

### L5 — NLRA Section 7 — Protected Concerted Activity / Retaliation (NLRB Charges 10-CA-381639, 10-CA-378814)
**Elements:** (1) concerted activity for mutual aid/protection re wages, safety, or working conditions; (2) employer knowledge; (3) adverse action; (4) anti-union/retaliatory motive or inherent interference.
**Our position:** Payroll/financial-stability discussions (April 15 leadership meeting; May 29 Weldon conversation), workplace-safety report (March 18), all protected; Dec 15 warning was retaliation; payroll failure proves selective enforcement (others not disciplined for same speech).
**Their position (anticipated):** Warning addressed false statements, not protected activity; business justification.
**Related factual issues:** F13, F14, F24, F25.

### L6 — Wage / Misclassification thread (FLSA & SC Payment of Wages) — *scope to confirm*
**Sub-issues:** (a) Feb 26–27 2026 **payroll failure** (timely payment of wages); (b) the 1099 **independent-contractor proposal / misclassification** question. 
**Status:** No standalone FLSA overtime filing located in the record; these currently live as **evidence/context** within L3/L5 and as a possible **SC Payment of Wages Act** angle. **Confirm with counsel** whether a separate wage claim is intended.
**Related factual issues:** F5, F14.

### L7 — Spoliation / Preservation of Evidence
**Issue:** Sept 2, 2025 email lockout — IT (Stuart Owen) allegedly instructed not to reset Lord's password, cutting access to records; formal preservation demand served Nov 14, 2025.
**Our position:** Adverse action + red flag for intentional evidence destruction; triggers preservation duty / adverse-inference argument.
**Related factual issues:** F18.

### L8 — Respondent Credibility / Material Misrepresentation
**Issue:** Position Statement (6/24/2026) repeatedly asserts Lord "is still employed … in her same position" — but she was terminated 5/15/2026, five weeks earlier.
**Our position:** A misstatement of the single most consequential fact undermines the reliability of the entire statement and evidences consciousness of liability.
**Related factual issues:** F17, F26.

### L9 — Parallel SC State-law claims
ADEA-parallel age claims under the **SC Human Affairs Law**; possible SC Payment of Wages Act (see L6). Referenced in the Formal Notice of Claims ("federal and state law"). **Confirm scope with counsel.**

## How evidence gets tagged (for DOCUMENT_INDEX)

Each evidence row carries:
- **Issue tags:** one or more codes (e.g., `L3;F13;F15`).
- **Direction:** `Supports` (helps us), `Adverse` (helps them), `Mixed`.
- **Weight:** `Direct` / `Strong` / `Corroborating` / `Background`.

This lets us answer instantly, for any legal or factual issue: *what do we have, which way does it cut, and how strong is it.*
